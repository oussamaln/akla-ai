# Visual QA Findings

Desktop and mobile verification confirms that the member dashboard, quest board, leaderboard, referral flow, profile editor, and administrator console retain their hierarchy, spacing, and responsive behavior at 1280px and 375px widths. The dark reputation-network design uses orbit rings for the score system, violet for primary action, mint for verified or active state, and gold for reward and multiplier emphasis.

The screens render meaningful empty states rather than blank panels. The member dashboard has clear score, multiplier, identity, and progression emphasis; the quest board remains readable on narrow screens; and the long-form administrator console stacks cleanly without horizontal loss of information. No blocking visual defects were observed in the captured routes.
